About the brand

A brand that lives and breathes cinema.

TRAMA is a food concept that brings a bold, playful identity to the movie-going experience. Inspired by cinematic nostalgia and pop culture, the brand blends retro influences with a contemporary aesthetic, creating a distinctive and recognizable visual universe.

The identity in context

Inspired by classic movie posters and pop culture, I developed a visual identity built around bold typography, vibrant colors and retro-inspired illustrations. The result is a cohesive and versatile brand system that comes to life through packaging, uniforms and digital applications, turning every touchpoint into part of the TRAMA experience.
